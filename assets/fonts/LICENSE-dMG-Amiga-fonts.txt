===================================================

These TrueType fonts in this folder are dMG's conversions, distributed unchanged and under
their original file names:

  Topaz_a500_v1.0.ttf        TopazPlus_a500_v1.0.ttf

  MicroKnight_v1.0.ttf       MicroKnightPlus_v1.0.ttf
